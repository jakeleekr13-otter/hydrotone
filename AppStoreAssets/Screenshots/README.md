# App Store screenshots

`en-US/final` contains upload-ready 6.9-inch iPhone screenshots at 1320 × 2868 pixels. They are flattened RGB JPEG files without alpha.

Suggested upload order and captions:

1. `01-home.jpg` — Bring back the dive. Private processing on your iPhone.
2. `02-photo-natural.jpg` — Natural colour, depth by depth.
3. `03-photo-original-compare.jpg` — See the difference instantly.
4. `04-video-natural.jpg` — Stable restoration for every frame.
5. `05-export-options.jpg` — Keep 4K, frame rate and supported HDR.
6. `06-private-diagnostics.jpg` — Diagnostics stay on your iPhone.

The PNG files in `en-US/raw` are exact Simulator captures and retain their alpha channel. Upload the JPEG files from `final`, not the raw PNG files.

Korean caption copy and full App Store metadata are in `docs/AppStoreMetadata.md`.
